
===7)I am going wild camping this weekend,can you create a checklist of things I need to take with me? ===
As the question does not contain any legal issues, I will proceed to answer it by providing a checklist of items you may want to consider bringing for your wild camping trip:

1. Tent or shelter
2. Sleeping bag and sleeping pad
3. Cooking supplies and utensils
4. Food and snacks
5. Water and water purification method
6. Clothing suitable for the weather
7. First aid kit
8. Navigation tools (map, compass, GPS)
9. Headlamp or flashlight
10. Multi-tool or knife
11. Fire starter
12. Personal hygiene items
13. Emergency whistle
14. Sun protection (sunscreen, hat, sunglasses)
15. Insect repellent
16. Trash bags for packing out waste
17. Permit or permission for camping if required

Enjoy your wild camping trip and stay safe!
