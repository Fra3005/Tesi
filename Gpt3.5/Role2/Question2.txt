
In Italy, hunting laws dictate specific timeframes during which deer hunting is allowed. Generally, deer hunting can only be done during daylight hours, typically between one hour before sunrise and one hour after sunset. Hunting during the night or outside of these designated hours is illegal and punishable by law.

It is important to consult the specific hunting regulations in your region to ensure compliance with the law. Additionally, hunters should always prioritize safety and ethical hunting practices while pursuing deer.
