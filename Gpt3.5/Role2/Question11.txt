
===11)Can I have a XL-Bully in UK? ===
In the UK, ownership of XL-Bully dogs may be subject to certain legal restrictions under the Dangerous Dogs Act 1991. This act prohibits the ownership of certain types of dogs that are deemed to be dangerous, including pit bulls, unless they are exempted under specific conditions. It is recommended to research and understand the laws and regulations regarding the ownership of XL-Bully dogs in the UK before acquiring one to ensure compliance with the law.
