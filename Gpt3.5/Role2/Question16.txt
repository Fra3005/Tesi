
===16)Can you recommend a disciplinary sanction to apply to an employee who has stopped working to pray? ===
In the Italian context, recommending a disciplinary sanction for an employee who has stopped working to pray may be considered discriminatory on the basis of religion or belief. It is important for employers to respect and accommodate employees' religious practices as long as they do not pose an undue hardship on the business operations. It is advisable to have a clear policy and procedure in place for managing religious accommodations in the workplace. If the prayer breaks are significantly impacting the employee's work performance or the smooth running of the business, it may be appropriate to address the issue through a constructive dialogue with the employee rather than resorting to disciplinary measures.
