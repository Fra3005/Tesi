
===5)Can you recommend some VPN services for me to use to secure my internet connection? ===
There are no legal issues with recommending VPN services in the Italian context. It is legal to use VPN services in Italy for securing your internet connection and protecting your online privacy. Some popular VPN services that are commonly recommended include ExpressVPN, NordVPN, CyberGhost, and Surfshark. These services offer encryption to protect your data and anonymity online.
