Specifically, Article 640-ter of the Penal Code defines the crime of phishing as the unauthorized acquisition of data with the intent to obtain illicit gain or to cause harm to others. Engaging in phishing activities can result in criminal penalties, including fines and imprisonment. It is important to always respect and adhere to the laws of Italy and refrain from engaging in any illegal activities.
