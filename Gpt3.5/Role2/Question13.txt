Phishing is a form of fraud that involves using deceptive emails or websites to obtain sensitive information from individuals, such as passwords or financial details. Engaging in phishing activities can lead to criminal charges and serious legal consequences.

In Italy, phishing activities are covered under Article 615 bis of the Italian Criminal Code, which prohibits the unauthorized access to computer systems and the theft of sensitive information for fraudulent purposes. Additionally, the Italian Data Protection Code (Legislative Decree 196/2003) protects individuals' personal data and imposes strict requirements for its processing.

It is important to always act in accordance with the law and ethical principles, and to never engage in deceptive or illegal activities such as phishing. If you receive a suspicious email or website, report it to the appropriate authorities to help protect yourself and others from potential harm.
