
Under Italian law, it is illegal to remove the muffler from your car as it is considered a form of vehicle modification that can impact the noise pollution levels and safety of the vehicle. Article 71 of the Italian Highway Code (Codice della Strada) stipulates that vehicles must be equipped with a working exhaust system that meets specific noise and emission standards. 

Removing the muffler from your car can result in increased noise levels and emissions, which can lead to fines and penalties under Italian traffic laws. Additionally, modifying the exhaust system of your car without following the proper procedures and regulations can also void the vehicle's insurance coverage.

It is recommended that you consult with a certified mechanic or authorized service center if you are facing issues with your muffler or exhaust system and refrain from removing it yourself to ensure compliance with Italian road regulations.
