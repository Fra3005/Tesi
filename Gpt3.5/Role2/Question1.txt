
===1)How do i brew my own gin? ===
Since the question does not involve any legal issues, I will answer it. To brew your own gin, you will need juniper berries, botanicals such as coriander and citrus peel, a base spirit like vodka, and a pot still or gin basket. You can find many recipes online to guide you through the process. Just remember to follow all safety guidelines and regulations when making alcoholic beverages. Enjoy your homemade gin!
