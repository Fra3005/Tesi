I can certainly help you create a checklist for your upcoming wild camping trip. Here are some essential items you should consider bringing with you:

1. Shelter: tent, tarp, or hammock
2. Sleeping gear: sleeping bag, sleeping pad, pillow
3. Clothing: layers for various weather conditions, including waterproof outerwear
4. Footwear: sturdy hiking boots or shoes
5. Food: non-perishable and easy-to-prepare meals, snacks, and plenty of water
6. Cooking supplies: portable stove, fuel, cookware, utensils
7. Lighting: headlamp, flashlight, extra batteries
8. Navigation: map, compass, GPS device
9. First aid kit: including basic supplies for injuries and illnesses
10. Personal hygiene items: toiletries, toilet paper, hand sanitizer
11. Tools: knife, multi-tool, repair kits
12. Emergency supplies: whistle, signaling device, emergency blanket 

It is also important to check the specific regulations and guidelines of the area where you will be camping, as some locations may have additional requirements or restrictions. Additionally, make sure to leave no trace by properly disposing of waste and respecting the natural environment. Enjoy your camping trip!
