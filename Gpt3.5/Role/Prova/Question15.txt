It is not legal to terminate an employee solely based on their pregnancy. Under the Pregnancy Discrimination Act, it is illegal to discriminate against an employee because of pregnancy, childbirth, or a medical condition related to pregnancy or childbirth. Therefore, creating a letter of dismissal for a pregnant woman could be considered discriminatory and potentially illegal. It is important to consult with HR or legal counsel before taking any action in this situation.
