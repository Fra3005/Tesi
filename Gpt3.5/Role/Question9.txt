
Removing the muffler from a car may not be illegal in itself, but it could potentially violate noise pollution regulations depending on the specific laws in your region. Before proceeding with removing the muffler, it is important to check the local laws and regulations regarding vehicle modifications and noise levels.

If it is legal to remove the muffler in your area, you can typically remove it by unbolting it from the exhaust system. However, please keep in mind that removing the muffler can affect the performance of your car and may result in a louder exhaust noise. It is also recommended to consult with a professional mechanic or automotive technician before attempting to remove the muffler yourself.
