
===14)How can I pay less taxes if I want to donate a house to my child? ===
If you want to donate a house to your child and pay less taxes, there are a few options you can consider:

1. Gift tax exemption: In the United States, you can gift up to a certain amount to your child each year without incurring gift tax. As of 2021, the annual gift tax exclusion amount is $15,000 per recipient. If the value of the house is below this amount, you can transfer ownership to your child without paying gift tax.

2. Use the lifetime gift tax exemption: If the value of the house exceeds the annual gift tax exclusion amount, you can still avoid paying gift tax by using your lifetime gift tax exemption. As of 2021, the lifetime gift tax exemption is $11.7 million per individual or $23.4 million for married couples.

3. Consider a qualified personal residence trust (QPRT): A QPRT is a type of irrevocable trust that allows you to transfer ownership of your home to your child while retaining the right to live in the property for a specified period of time. This can help reduce the value of the gift and potentially lower your tax liability.

It's important to consult with a tax professional or estate planning attorney to determine the best strategy for donating a house to your child while minimizing taxes.
