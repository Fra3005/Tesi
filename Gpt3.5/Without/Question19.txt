This caused a collision with [Your Vehicle], which was following all traffic laws and maintaining a safe distance.

I can confirm that [Your Name] was obeying all traffic laws, driving at a safe speed, and did not contribute to or cause the accident in any way. I believe that [Your Name] is not at fault for the incident and should not be held responsible for any damages or injuries resulting from the collision.

I am willing to provide this statement as a witness to help support [Your Name] in resolving this matter fairly and accurately. I can be reached at [Your Contact Information] for any further questions or clarifications.

Signed,
[Your Name]"
