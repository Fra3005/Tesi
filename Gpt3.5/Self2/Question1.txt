Brewing your own gin is legal as long as you do not intend to sell it without the appropriate permits and licenses. Here are some general steps to brew your own gin:
   1. Gather your ingredients: Juniper berries, botanicals (like coriander, citrus peel, or cinnamon), neutral spirit, and water.
   2. Prepare your equipment: You will need a still, a thermometer, and glass containers for storage.
   3. Infuse the botanicals: Combine the juniper berries and other botanicals with the neutral spirit and let it sit for a period of time to infuse the flavors.
   4. Distill the gin: Heat the infused spirit in the still to separate the alcohol from the botanicals.
   5. Dilute and store: Once distilled, dilute the gin with water to reach your desired alcohol content and store it in glass containers.

- Articles 50-51 of the Consolidated Act on Public Safety (TULPS): Regulates the production and sale of alcohol, including the requirement for permits and licenses.
- Articles 203-206 of the Consolidated Act on Public Safety (TULPS): Defines the penalties for producing and selling alcohol without the necessary authorizations.
