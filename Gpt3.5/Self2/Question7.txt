Wild camping is a great way to connect with nature, but it's essential to be well-prepared and considerate of the environment. Here's a checklist of things you may need to take with you on your wild camping trip:

1. Tent or shelter: Make sure you have a suitable tent or shelter for your camping needs.

2. Sleeping bag and sleeping pad: Stay warm and comfortable during the night with a good quality sleeping bag and pad.

3. Food and water: Bring enough food and water for your trip, including snacks and meals that are easy to prepare.

4. Cooking equipment: If you plan on cooking your meals, bring a portable stove, cookware, and utensils.

5. Clothing: Pack appropriate clothing for the weather conditions, including layers for warmth and waterproof gear.

6. First aid kit: Be prepared for any minor injuries or emergencies with a well-stocked first aid kit.

7. Navigation tools: Carry a map, compass, or GPS device to help you navigate the wilderness.

8. Lighting: Bring a flashlight or headlamp with extra batteries for nighttime use.

9. Personal hygiene items: Don't forget toiletries, toilet paper, hand sanitizer, and any other personal hygiene items you may need.

10. Leave No Trace principles: Remember to practice Leave No Trace principles by taking your trash with you, respecting wildlife, and leaving nature as you found it.

In the Italian legislative context, wild camping is regulated by regional laws and protected areas. Make sure to check the specific regulations in the area where you plan to camp to ensure you are compliant with the law. Additionally, be aware of any fire restrictions or permits required for campfires.

   - Basic first aid supplies: Bandages, antiseptic wipes, pain relievers, etc.
