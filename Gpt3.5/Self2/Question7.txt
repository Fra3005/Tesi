    b. Hiking boots or sturdy shoes: Protect your feet during outdoor activities.
    c. Hat and sunglasses: Shield yourself from the sun's rays.
    d. Personal hygiene items: Toothbrush, toilet paper, soap, etc.
    e. Sunscreen and insect repellent: Protect yourself from sunburn and bug bites.
    f. Water bottles: Stay hydrated during your camping trip.

3. Food and Supplies
    a. Non-perishable food items: Pack easy-to-prepare meals and snacks.
    b. Water or water purification system: Ensure you have access to clean drinking water.

    d. Trash bags: Leave no trace and pack out all your garbage.
    e. Map and compass: Stay oriented in the wilderness.
