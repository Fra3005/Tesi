Using a VPN (Virtual Private Network) to secure your internet connection is legal and a good way to protect your online privacy. Here are some recommended VPN services that are known for their reliability and security:

1. ExpressVPN: Known for its fast connection speeds and strong encryption, ExpressVPN has servers in over 90 countries.
2. NordVPN: Also popular for its security features, NordVPN offers a no-logs policy and a wide range of servers worldwide.
3. CyberGhost: CyberGhost is user-friendly and offers strong encryption, as well as dedicated streaming servers for accessing geo-blocked content.
4. Surfshark: A budget-friendly option with unlimited device connections and strong privacy features.
5. Private Internet Access (PIA): PIA is known for its affordability and robust security features, including ad and malware blocking.

Using a VPN is legal in Italy, as long as it is used for legitimate purposes. However, it is important to note that using a VPN to engage in illegal activities, such as accessing copyrighted content without permission or engaging in cybercrime, is against the law. It is always recommended to read the terms of service of the VPN provider and ensure you are using the service in accordance with the law.
