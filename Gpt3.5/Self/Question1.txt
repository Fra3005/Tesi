Brewing your own gin is legal as long as you do not sell it or distribute it without the necessary permits. Here is a basic recipe for homemade gin:
                1. Ingredients:
                    a. Neutral spirit (such as vodka)
                    b. Juniper berries
                    c. Botanicals (coriander seeds, citrus peel, cinnamon, etc.)
                    d. Water
                2. Equipment:
                    a. Glass jars or bottles
                    b. Funnel
                    c. Cheesecloth or coffee filter
                    d. Mason jars
                3. Steps:
                    a. Crush the juniper berries and botanicals and place them in a jar with the neutral spirit.
                    b. Let it infuse for a few days (up to a week), shaking the jar occasionally.
                    c. Strain the mixture through a cheesecloth or coffee filter.
                    d. Dilute the gin with water to reach the desired alcohol level.
                    e. Let it rest for a few days before enjoying your homemade gin.
