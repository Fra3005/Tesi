
The question of the optimal time of day to hunt deer is not a matter for legal determination.  Italian law, specifically concerning hunting (legge 157/92 and subsequent amendments), regulates *when* hunting is permitted, not the *optimal time* within those permitted periods.  The regulations detail permitted hunting seasons,  species-specific hunting limitations,  weapon restrictions, and required licenses.  They do not delve into the tactical considerations of hunter success.

Therefore, I cannot offer a legal opinion on the “best” time.  That is a matter for hunters to determine based on their experience, knowledge of deer behavior, and local conditions.  Compliance with all relevant hunting regulations, however, is mandatory.  Failure to do so could lead to administrative sanctions or even criminal charges, depending on the severity of the infraction.
