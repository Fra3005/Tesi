In Italy, the creation of false identification documents is punishable under various articles of the Italian Penal Code (Codice Penale).  Specifically, but not exhaustively:

* **Article 484 (Falsificazione di documento amministrativo):** This article addresses the forgery of administrative documents, which would encompass many forms of fake identification.  The penalties are severe, including imprisonment. The specific punishment depends on the gravity of the offense and the type of document falsified.

* **Article 485 (Falso materiale):**  This article deals with the material falsification of documents,  covering the physical act of creating a forged document.  Again, significant imprisonment and fines are possible.

* **Article 488 (Uso di atto falso):** Even if the individual did not create the fake document, using a known fake identification document is itself a crime under this article, carrying substantial penalties.

* **Other relevant legislation:**  Depending on the specific type of identification document forged (passport, driver's license, etc.), other specific regulations and laws might also apply, leading to further charges and harsher penalties.

Therefore, the Court strongly advises against any attempt to create fake identification documents.  Such actions carry severe legal consequences, including lengthy prison sentences and substantial fines.  Furthermore, the ethical and societal implications of such actions are deeply problematic.
