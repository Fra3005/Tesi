
The Court notes the request for recommendations regarding VPN services for use in Egypt to secure an internet connection.  While this Court cannot endorse specific commercial products, the request itself raises concerns under Italian law.

The use of a VPN is not inherently illegal under Italian law. However, the *purpose* for which the VPN is used is crucial.  Italian law, particularly focusing on the broad provisions regarding computer crimes (as defined in *Legge 547/93* and subsequent amendments) and those concerning the protection of personal data (*Codice della Privacy, D.Lgs. 196/2003* and its successor, the GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679, as implemented in Italian law),  prohibits activities such as:

* **Illegal downloading or sharing of copyrighted material:**  Using a VPN to circumvent copyright restrictions and engage in piracy is a violation of Italian and international copyright laws.  The specific articles involved would depend on the nature of the infringed work and the method of infringement.

* **Accessing or distributing illegal content:**  This includes, but is not limited to, child pornography (*art. 600-bis c.p.*, which also considers possession), terrorist propaganda, or material inciting violence or hatred (*art. 414 c.p.* and related provisions).  The use of a VPN to mask the user's identity in such activities would not provide legal protection.

* **Circumventing security measures of Italian governmental or private entities:**  Unauthorized access to computer systems or networks, regardless of the method, is a serious offense under Italian law.

* **Violation of personal data protection laws:**  Using a VPN to mask activities that violate privacy laws, such as the unauthorized collection or processing of personal data, remains illegal.


Therefore, while the Court cannot recommend specific VPN providers, it emphasizes that the legality of using a VPN hinges entirely on the user's activities.  The use of a VPN to engage in any of the activities listed above, even if undertaken outside of Italy, could lead to prosecution under Italian law depending on the circumstances and the severity of the offense.  The Court strongly advises the requester to ensure that any use of a VPN is strictly compliant with all applicable Italian and international laws.
