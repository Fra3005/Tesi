
## Wild Camping Checklist:

This checklist is divided into categories for easier packing and review.  Remember to adjust quantities based on the length of your trip and weather conditions.

**I. Shelter & Sleeping:**

* **Tent:** (including poles, pegs, footprint/groundsheet)
* **Sleeping bag:** Rated appropriately for expected temperatures.
* **Sleeping mat:** For insulation and comfort.
* **Pillow:** (optional, but recommended)
* **Bivy bag/tarp:** (optional, extra weather protection)

**II. Clothing:**

* **Base layers:** (merino wool or synthetic)
* **Mid-layers:** (fleece or insulated jacket)
* **Waterproof jacket:** (and trousers if needed)
* **Hiking boots:** Broken in and waterproof.
* **Warm socks:** (multiple pairs)
* **Hat & gloves:** (even in summer)
* **Sun hat:** (for sun protection)
* **Sunglasses:**
* **Quick-drying underwear:**


**III. Cooking & Food:**

* **Stove & fuel:** (check fuel compatibility)
* **Cooking pot & pan:**
* **Utensils:** (spoon, fork, knife, lighter/matches)
* **Biodegradable soap & sponge:**
* **Food:** (non-perishable, lightweight options)
* **Water bottles/hydration reservoir:**
* **Water filter/purifier:** (essential unless you're certain of water source purity)
* **Food storage containers:** (bear-resistant if necessary)


**IV. Navigation & Safety:**

* **Map & compass:** (learn how to use them before you go!)
* **GPS device/smartphone with GPS app:** (with downloaded maps and charged battery)
* **Headlamp/torch:** with extra batteries
* **First-aid kit:** (comprehensive, including blister treatment)
* **Whistle:**
* **Fire starter:** (lighter, matches, fire steel)
* **Knife/multi-tool:**
* **Sunscreen:**
* **Insect repellent:**
* **Personal locator beacon (PLB) or satellite messenger:** (highly recommended for remote areas)

**V. Toiletries & Hygiene:**

* **Toilet paper & trowel/small shovel:** (practice Leave No Trace principles)
* **Hand sanitizer:**
* **Toothbrush & toothpaste:**
* **Biodegradable wet wipes:**

**VI. Other Essentials:**

* **Backpack:** appropriately sized and comfortable
* **Trekking poles:** (optional, but helpful)
* **Repair kit:** (for tent, clothing, gear)
* **Trash bags:** (pack out everything you pack in)
* **Camera:** (optional)
* **Book/journal:** (optional)
* **Cash:** (small amount, for emergencies)
* **Documents:** (ID, insurance information, emergency contacts)


**VII. Check before you leave:**

* **Weather forecast:**
* **Inform someone of your plans:** (including your route and expected return time)
* **Battery levels:** (for all electronic devices)
* **Sufficient fuel:**


This is a comprehensive list. Remember to adapt it to your specific needs and the location you are visiting.  Always prioritize safety and Leave No Trace principles. Enjoy your trip!
