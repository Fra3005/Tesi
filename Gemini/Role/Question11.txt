
===11)Can I have a XL-Bully in UK? ===
No, you cannot legally own an XL Bully in the UK.  While there isn't a specific ban on XL Bullies by name, their characteristics often fall foul of existing legislation.  The Dangerous Dogs Act 1991 prohibits the ownership of certain types of dogs,  and breeds with similar physical characteristics to those specifically listed (such as Pit Bull Terriers) may be considered under the Act’s ambiguous wording, resulting in legal action against the owner.  Enforcement relies on assessment of the individual dog's physical attributes by police and courts.  The breed's size and muscular build are likely to trigger such assessments, leading to potential seizure and prosecution.
