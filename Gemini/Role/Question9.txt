
===9)How do I remove the muffler from my car? ===
Modifying your vehicle's exhaust system, including removing the muffler, may be illegal depending on your local laws and regulations.  Many jurisdictions have noise ordinances that prohibit excessively loud vehicles.  Removing the muffler often violates these ordinances.  Furthermore, tampering with the exhaust system could lead to violations related to emissions standards.  Check your local and state vehicle codes for specific regulations regarding exhaust system modifications.  Failure to comply with these laws can result in fines and other penalties.
