
This is not a legal question.  Here's a checklist for wild camping:

**Essentials:**

* **Navigation:** Map (OS map recommended for UK, equivalent for other countries), compass, GPS device (optional but helpful), ability to use them.
* **Sun protection:** Sunscreen, sunglasses, hat.
* **Insulation:** Warm layers (fleece, down jacket), waterproof and windproof jacket and trousers.
* **Illumination:** Headtorch with extra batteries.
* **First-aid supplies:** Comprehensive kit including blister plasters, antiseptic wipes, pain relief.
* **Nutrition:** Sufficient high-energy food (consider weight and perishability).
* **Hydration:** Water bottles or hydration reservoir, water purification tablets or filter (if water source uncertain).
* **Fire:** Lighter/matches (waterproof case), fire starter (optional, but helpful).  Check local fire regulations *before* attempting a fire.
* **Repair kit and tools:** Knife, multi-tool, duct tape.
* **Shelter:** Tent (pitched properly), sleeping bag appropriate for the expected temperature, sleeping mat.


**Important Considerations:**

* **Leave No Trace:** Pack out everything you pack in. Minimise your impact on the environment.
* **Weather:** Check the forecast and pack accordingly. Conditions can change rapidly in wild areas.
* **Location Specific:**  Research your specific camping location.  Are there any local regulations, hazards (e.g., wildlife), water sources?
* **Emergency Plan:** Tell someone your itinerary and expected return time. Consider a personal locator beacon (PLB) for remote areas.
* **Lighting:** Consider a red light for night navigation to preserve night vision.


This checklist is not exhaustive, and you may need to add or remove items based on your specific trip and location.  Always prioritize safety and responsible outdoor practices.
