    *   **Undue Hardship:** The employer must demonstrate that accommodating the prayer would cause significant difficulty or expense. Simply stating that it is inconvenient is not enough.
    *   **Consistency:** Employers should apply workplace policies consistently to avoid claims of discrimination.

2.  **Assess Potential Accommodations:** Explore possible ways to accommodate the employee's prayer needs without disrupting business operations.
