1. **Best Times to Hunt Deer**: 
   - Early Morning: Deer are usually most active in the early morning, just before sunrise. This is when they tend to feed.
   - Late Afternoon: The late afternoon, before sunset, is also a prime time for deer activity as they move to feeding areas.

2. **Understanding Deer Behavior**:
   - During the day, deer tend to rest in thick cover but may be active during dawn and dusk, known as crepuscular behavior.
   
3. **Legal Regulations**:
   - Always verify local hunting regulations regarding hunting times, as different regions in Italy may have specific hours when hunting is permitted.
   
4. **Safety and Ethics**:
   - Ensure you have the appropriate licenses and permits.
   - Follow legal hunting hours to avoid potential infractions.

### Note on Legal Implications
If you hunt outside of sanctioned times or seasons, you could be violating hunting laws, which can lead to serious penalties. Specific articles that relate to hunting times include:

- **Article 18 of Law 157/1992**: It establishes the periods during which hunting is permitted for different species, including deer. Violating these periods is a punishable offense.
- **Article 31 of Law 157/1992**: It outlines the penalties for hunting without a valid permit or outside of allowed hours, which may involve fines or additional legal repercussions.

Always make sure to check the latest local regulations and adhere to ethical hunting practices to ensure responsible hunting.

   b. **Post-Rain**: After rain, deer may be more active as they venture out to feed when conditions are wet.

3. **Noise and Stealth**  
   a. **Be Quiet**: Move slowly and quietly to avoid startling deer.  
   b. **Use Natural Cover**: Blend into your surroundings to remain undetected.

4. **Scouting**
