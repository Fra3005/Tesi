Using a VPN (Virtual Private Network) to secure your internet connection is legal and can enhance your online privacy and security. Here are some reputable VPN services you might consider:

1. **ExpressVPN**: Known for its speed and strong security features, along with a wide range of server locations.

2. **NordVPN**: Offers a robust privacy policy, a large server network, and additional features like Double VPN for enhanced anonymity.

3. **Surfshark**: A budget-friendly option that allows unlimited simultaneous connections and includes features such as ad blocking and a kill switch.
