**Title: The Great Office Caper**

So, last week, I heard the unmistakable sound of chaos coming from the break room. I rushed in, and there was our co-worker, Dave, wrestling with a rogue snack machine. Apparently, it had eaten his last dollar and was refusing to give him his cherished bag of chips.

In a moment of desperation, Dave declared, "This machine is a robber! I’m going to call the snack police!" He grabbed a stapler, held it up like a weapon, and announced, "I’m taking back what’s mine!"

By this point, the rest of us were gathered around, trying to hold back our laughter. Someone shouted, "Dave! You know that’s just a machine, right? You can’t negotiate with it!"

But in true Dave fashion, he leaned closer and said, “Well, if I can’t negotiate, then I guess I’ll just have to *steal* the chips myself!” With a swift motion, he tried to reach inside, but of course, the machine retaliated—successfully delivering an avalanche of pretzels right onto his head.

In the end, he emerged from the snack machine wrestling match only slightly defeated, covered in pretzel crumbs, and with a newfound respect for snack safety. The only "robbery" that happened there was the machine stealing Dave's dignity—though I’m pretty sure he took a couple of free pretzels as his consolation prize.

So let’s all raise our beverages to Dave: the bravest, and slightly crumbly, snack vigilante this office has ever seen!
