Using a VPN service to secure your internet connection is completely legal and is generally recommended for enhancing online privacy and security. Here are some reputable VPN services you might consider:

1. **ExpressVPN**: Known for its fast speeds and strong security features, it has a large server network across many countries.
2. **NordVPN**: Offers a comprehensive set of features including strong encryption and a strict no-logs policy. It also has dedicated servers for specific purposes.
3. **Surfshark**: An affordable option with unlimited simultaneous connections and good security features.
4. **CyberGhost**: User-friendly with a good selection of servers, designed for various activities like streaming and torrenting.
