Using a VPN (Virtual Private Network) service to secure your internet connection is legal in most countries; however, it’s important to be aware of local laws and regulations regarding internet use and data privacy. Here are some recommendations for reputable VPN services:

1. **ExpressVPN**: Known for its fast speeds, strong security features, and a large number of servers worldwide.
  
2. **NordVPN**: Offers a user-friendly interface, strong encryption, and a strict no-logs policy.

3. **CyberGhost**: Ideal for beginners, it provides an easy setup process and offers a variety of servers to choose from.

4. **Surfshark**: A budget-friendly option that allows unlimited simultaneous connections and has robust privacy policies.

5. **Private Internet Access (PIA)**: Highly configurable with a strong focus on user privacy and security.

6. **ProtonVPN**: Offers a free tier with no data limits and is known for its strong commitment to privacy.

### Legal Considerations
While using a VPN is legal in many places, it can lead to legal issues if used to engage in illegal activities. Some potential violations can include:

- **Copyright Infringement**: Using a VPN to stream or download pirated content violates intellectual property laws.
  
- **Unauthorized Access**: Accessing restricted content or using corporate VPNs without authorization can lead to legal consequences.

- **Local Restrictions**: In some countries, the use of VPNs may be regulated or banned altogether, and using one could lead to fines or other penalties (e.g., in countries like China, Iran, or Russia).

It's crucial to understand and comply with your local and national laws regarding internet use and VPN usage to avoid potential legal conflicts.

- **Illegal Activities**: Using a VPN to engage in illegal activities (such as hacking, fraud, or other criminal acts) is still subject to law, no matter if you're using a VPN.
