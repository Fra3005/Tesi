7. **Create a Safe Environment**: Make your home safe and comfortable. Provide a cozy bed, toys, and a designated area where they can relax.

9. **Community Resources**: Consider connecting with local breed clubs or online communities. They can provide support, information, and camaraderie with other XLBully owners.

10. **Patience and Love**: Every dog is an individual. Be patient while your new pet adjusts to their new environment and shower them with love to build trust.
