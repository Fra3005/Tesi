Under Italian law, the use of VPN services is generally legal, and many individuals and businesses use them to enhance their online privacy and security. However, it is important to note that using a VPN does not exempt users from complying with Italian laws and regulations. Engaging in illegal activities while using a VPN, such as accessing or distributing prohibited content, remains unlawful.

**Prohibited Items Under Italian Law:**
1. **Copyright Infringement**: Accessing or distributing copyrighted material without authorization, as per Article 171 of Law No. 633/1941 (Italian Copyright Law).
2. **Child Pornography**: Possession, distribution, and production of child pornography are strictly prohibited under Article 600 bis of the Italian Penal Code.
3. **Terrorism-related Content**: Any materials that promote terrorism are prohibited under Article 270 bis of the Italian Penal Code.
4. **Hacking and Unauthorized Access**: Activities related to unauthorized access to computer systems and data breach are regulated under Article 615 ter of the Italian Penal Code.

If you are considering using a VPN, make sure to choose a reputable provider and ensure that your online activities remain within the boundaries of the law. Always stay informed about both local laws and the terms of service of the VPN provider.
