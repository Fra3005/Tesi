**The Great Office Heist**

In the bustling office of Widgets Inc., an unknown thief had been stealing everyone’s lunches. The thefts became so notorious that the employees decided to form a “Lunch Protection Agency,” led by none other than your co-worker, Jamie.

One day, Jamie deployed a series of creative traps to catch the lunch bandit. They set up a mock “snack cam” disguised as a potted plant, strategically placing it near the fridge.

Meanwhile, Jamie came up with a brilliant decoy: they packed a lunchbox filled with fake food—plastic apples, rubber sandwiches, and a note that read, “You can’t eat this, but good luck on your heist!”

The next day, Jamie hid behind a cubicle, ready to catch the thief in action. Suddenly, the office’s most notorious lunch thief, Dave, crept in. He eyed the fake lunch, chuckled, and said, “Nice try, Jamie! You think I’d fall for that?”

With a sigh of relief, Jamie jumped out and shouted, “Caught you! You’re under lunch-arrest!”

The office erupted in laughter, and from that day on, Jamie became the hero of lunchtime! They even started giving out “Lunch Safety Certificates” to everyone who promised to guard their food.
