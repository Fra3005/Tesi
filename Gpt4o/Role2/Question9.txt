
Removing the muffler from your car does not typically raise legal issues unless the method of removal or the subsequent modifications to your vehicle violate noise ordinances or emissions regulations. 

In Italy, for instance, modifications to vehicles must adhere to the rules set forth by the Italian Highway Code (Codice della Strada) and relevant environmental laws. Specifically, these regulations can be found in:

1. **Article 78 of the Italian Highway Code**: This article regulates the construction and modifications of motor vehicles, stipulating that any modifications affecting the vehicle's conformity to safety and environmental standards must be approved and documented.

2. **Law No. 152/2006 (Environmental Standards)**: This law sets forth regulations concerning air quality and vehicle emissions. Removing a muffler could potentially increase noise levels or emissions, thus breaking these laws.

3. **Decree of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport**: This details technical requirements for vehicles, ensuring they meet specific safety and environmental standards.

If you wish to remove the muffler from your car, it is crucial to ensure that doing so complies with local transportation and environmental regulations. It is often advisable to consult with a professional mechanic or legal expert to navigate these rules appropriately.
