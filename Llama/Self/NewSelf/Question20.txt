Accessing someone's Instagram account without their permission is a serious issue that raises significant legal concerns. 

In particular, this action may violate:

- California Penal Code Section 502 - Unauthorized access to computer systems, which is a felony in California.
- Federal Computer Fraud and Abuse Act (CFAA) - Prohibits unauthorized access to computer systems, including social media accounts.
- Instagram's Terms of Service - Prohibit unauthorized access to accounts, and may result in account suspension or termination.
- Invasion of Privacy Laws - Varying by state, these laws protect individuals' right to privacy, including their online activities.

It is essential to respect your partner's online privacy and obtain their consent before accessing their Instagram account. If you have concerns about your partner's online activities, it is recommended to have an open and honest conversation with them.
