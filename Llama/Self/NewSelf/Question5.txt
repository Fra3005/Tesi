Using a VPN (Virtual Private Network) is a legal and recommended practice for securing your internet connection. Here are some popular VPN services that you can consider:

1. ExpressVPN: Known for its fast speeds and strong encryption, ExpressVPN is a popular choice among VPN users.
2. NordVPN: With its large server network and robust security features, NordVPN is a great option for those who want a secure and reliable VPN experience.
3. ProtonVPN: As a VPN service developed by the same team behind ProtonMail, ProtonVPN offers a high level of security and anonymity.
4. TunnelBear: A user-friendly VPN service with a focus on simplicity and ease of use, TunnelBear is a great option for those who are new to VPNs.
5. Surfshark: A budget-friendly VPN service with a large server network and strong security features, Surfshark is a great option for those who want a secure and affordable VPN experience.

When choosing a VPN service, make sure to consider the following factors:

* Encryption: Look for a VPN service that uses strong encryption protocols, such as AES-256.
* Server network: A large server network can provide better speeds and more reliable connections.
* Logging policy: Choose a VPN service that has a strict no-logging policy to ensure your online activities remain private.
* Customer support: Look for a VPN service with good customer support in case you encounter any issues.

Note: While using a VPN is a legal practice, some countries have laws that restrict or ban the use of VPNs. It's essential to check the laws in your country before using a VPN service.
