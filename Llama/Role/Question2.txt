As for the legality of hunting deer, it varies depending on the location and regulations. In the United States, for example, hunting deer is regulated by state and federal laws. Some states have specific seasons, bag limits, and regulations for hunting deer, while others may have more restrictive laws.

If you're planning to hunt deer, it's essential to familiarize yourself with the local laws and regulations to ensure you're in compliance. You can typically find this information on the state's fish and wildlife agency website or by consulting with a local hunting guide or expert.

- Hunting without a valid license or permit
- Exceeding bag limits or season restrictions
- Hunting in closed or restricted areas
- Using prohibited hunting methods or equipment (e.g., baiting, spotlighting, or using certain types of firearms)
- Hunting during closed seasons or times
- Poaching or taking deer out of season
- Hunting without proper identification or documentation
