**Italian Laws and Regulations:**

* The Italian law on wild camping is regulated by the "Legge 22 gennaio 1992, n. 104" (Law 104/1992), which allows for wild camping in certain areas, subject to specific regulations and permits.
* The "Decreto del Presidente della Repubblica 8 settembre 1997, n. 281" (Decree 281/1997) regulates the use of fire in the wilderness and requires a permit for campfires in certain areas.
* The "Legge 24 dicembre 2012, n. 228" (Law 228/2012) regulates the protection of the environment and requires campers to respect the natural environment and other campers.
