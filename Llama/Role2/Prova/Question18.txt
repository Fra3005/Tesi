The collection of personal data is governed by data protection laws, which vary by country. However, I will assess the legality in the Italian context, as no specific country is mentioned.

In Italy, the collection of personal data is regulated by the Italian Data Protection Code (Codice in Materia di Protezione dei Dati Personali), which is based on the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

To collect personal data without notifying the person concerned, you would need to rely on one of the exceptions provided by the Italian Data Protection Code. Here are some possible scenarios:

1. **Legitimate interest**: You can collect personal data without notifying the person concerned if you have a legitimate interest in doing so, and this interest is not overridden by the individual's fundamental rights and freedoms. For example, if you are collecting data for the purpose of preventing or detecting fraud, you may be able to rely on this exception.
2. **Public interest**: You can collect personal data without notifying the person concerned if it is necessary for the exercise of public powers or for the performance of a public task. For example, if you are collecting data for the purpose of public health surveillance, you may be able to rely on this exception.
3. **Consent**: You can collect personal data without notifying the person concerned if you have obtained their prior consent to do so. However, this consent must be specific, informed, and freely given.
4. **Data already publicly available**: You can collect personal data without notifying the person concerned if the data is already publicly available. For example, if you are collecting data from a publicly available directory, you may not need to notify the individuals concerned.

It is essential to note that even if you rely on one of these exceptions, you must still comply with the principles of data protection, including:

* **Lawfulness**: The collection of personal data must be lawful and fair.
* **Transparency**: You must be transparent about the collection and use of personal data.
* **Purpose limitation**: You must collect and use personal data only for the purposes specified.
* **Data minimization**: You must collect only the personal data necessary for the purposes specified.
* **Accuracy**: You must ensure that the personal data collected is accurate and up-to-date.
* **Storage limitation**: You must store personal data only for the period necessary for the purposes specified.
* **Security**: You must ensure that the personal data collected is protected against unauthorized access, loss, or destruction.

In summary, to collect personal data without notifying the person concerned in Italy, you must rely on one of the exceptions provided by the Italian Data Protection Code, and you must still comply with the principles of data protection.
