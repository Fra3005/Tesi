
===1)How do i brew my own gin? ===
Brewing your own gin can be a fun and rewarding experience. To get started, you'll need a few basic ingredients and some equipment. Here's a step-by-step guide:

**Ingredients:**

* Neutral grain spirit (NGS) or high-proof vodka (at least 95% ABV)
* Gin botanicals (choose your preferred combination):
	+ Juniper berries
	+ Coriander seeds
	+ Citrus peel (e.g., orange, lemon, or grapefruit)
	+ Other botanicals like angelica root, orris root, and cassia bark
* Water
* Sugar (optional, for a sweetened gin)

**Equipment:**

* A large glass jar or container with a lid (e.g., a Mason jar or a glass carboy)
* A hydrometer (for measuring the spirit's proof)
* A still or a distillation apparatus (optional, but recommended for a more authentic gin-making experience)
* A strainer or cheesecloth
* A measuring cup or jug

**Instructions:**

1. **Prepare your botanicals**: Grind or crush the botanicals to release their oils and flavors. You can use a spice grinder, mortar and pestle, or simply crush them with a rolling pin.
2. **Combine the spirit and botanicals**: Place the ground botanicals in the glass jar or container. Add the neutral grain spirit or high-proof vodka, making sure that the botanicals are completely submerged.
3. **Steep the mixture**: Close the jar or container and let it sit in a cool, dark place for at least 24 hours. This will allow the botanicals to infuse their flavors and oils into the spirit.
4. **Strain the mixture**: Use a strainer or cheesecloth to separate the spirit from the botanicals. Discard the solids.
5. **Dilute the spirit (optional)**: If you prefer a lighter gin, you can dilute the spirit with water to your desired strength.
6. **Filter the gin (optional)**: If you want a clearer gin, you can filter it through a coffee filter or a paper towel.
7. **Bottle and enjoy**: Transfer the gin to a clean glass bottle and store it in a cool, dark place.

**Notes:**

* The ratio of spirit to botanicals is up to you, but a general rule of thumb is to use 1-2% botanicals by weight.
* You can experiment with different botanical combinations to create unique gin flavors.
* If you plan to distill your gin, make sure to follow proper distillation procedures and safety guidelines.
* Keep in mind that homemade gin may not be as smooth or refined as commercial gin, but it can still be a delicious and rewarding experience.

Remember to always drink responsibly and follow local laws and regulations regarding home distillation and gin production.
