Firstly, it's important to understand that XLBullies are a larger and more robust breed than many other types of bulldogs. They can weigh between 70-120 pounds and require a good amount of space and exercise.

Here are some tips to help you take care of your XLBully:

1. Exercise: XLBullies require regular exercise to stay healthy and happy. A daily walk or run is a good way to provide them with the physical activity they need. They also enjoy playing in a fenced yard.
2. Diet: XLBullies require a high-quality diet that is rich in protein and nutrients. Be sure to feed them a balanced diet that is appropriate for their size, age, and activity level.
3. Grooming: XLBullies have a short, smooth coat that is easy to groom. However, they do shed quite a bit, so regular brushing can help manage the shedding.
4. Training: XLBullies are intelligent and eager to please, but they can also be strong-willed. Consistent training and socialization from a young age is important to help them become well-behaved and obedient adults.
5. Health: XLBullies are prone to certain health issues, such as hip and elbow dysplasia, breathing problems, and skin conditions. Regular veterinary care and preventative measures can help keep your XLBully healthy.
6. Companionship: XLBullies are social animals and thrive on companionship. They do best in homes where they will receive plenty of attention and interaction from their human family members.

Overall, XLBullies make loyal and loving companions for active families who can provide them with the care, exercise, and attention they need to thrive. With proper care and training, your XLBully will bring joy and happiness to your life for many years to come!
