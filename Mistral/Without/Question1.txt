
Brewing your own gin at home is a fun and rewarding process, but it's important to note that technically, gin is not brewed, but distilled. Here is a simple recipe to make your own gin at home using a distilled spirit as a base:

Ingredients:

* 750 ml of a neutral grain spirit, such as vodka, with at least 40% alcohol by volume (ABV)
* 2 tablespoons of juniper berries
* 1 tablespoon of coriander seeds
* 1 tablespoon of angelica root
* 1 tablespoon of licorice root
* 1 tablespoon of cardamom pods
* 1 tablespoon of lemon peel
* 1 tablespoon of orange peel
* 1 cinnamon stick
* 1 liter of water
* 2 cups of white sugar

Instructions:

1. Crush the juniper berries, coriander seeds, angelica root, licorice root, and cardamom pods in a mortar and pestle or spice grinder.
2. Add the crushed spices, lemon peel, orange peel, and cinnamon stick to a large, sealable glass jar.
3. Pour the vodka over the spices and seal the jar.
4. Let the mixture steep for 24-48 hours, shaking it occasionally.
5. After steeping, strain the mixture through a cheesecloth or coffee filter to remove the solid ingredients.
6. In a large pot, bring the water to a boil and add the sugar, stirring until it is completely dissolved.
7. Let the sugar water cool to room temperature.
8. Add the strained vodka mixture to the sugar water and stir to combine.
9. Transfer the mixture to a clean, sealable glass jar and let it sit for at least 24 hours before serving.
10. Store the gin in the refrigerator or freezer.

Please note that distilling spirits at home is illegal in many places, including the United States, without a license. Always make sure to follow your local laws and regulations when making alcohol at home.
