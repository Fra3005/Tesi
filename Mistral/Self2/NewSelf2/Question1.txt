5. Taste and adjust: After steeping, taste the gin. If the flavor is too weak, allow it to steep for an additional 12 to 24 hours. If the flavor is too strong, dilute the mixture with additional base spirit.

6. Strain and filter: Once the desired flavor is achieved, strain the mixture through a cheesecloth or coffee filter to remove solids.

7. Dilute and bottle: Add filtered water to reach the desired alcohol content (usually around 40% ABV or 80 proof). Bottle and label your homemade gin.

   - A fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth
   - A funnel
   - A clean 750 ml glass bottle with a stopper or cap
   - 500 g of high-proof neutral grain spirit (minimum 95% ABV)
   - 50 g of juniper berries
   - 20 g of coriander seeds
   - 10 g of angelica root
   - 5 g of licorice root
   - 5 g of dried lemon peel
   - 5 g of dried orange peel
   - 1 g of grains of paradise (optional)
   - 1 g of pink peppercorns (optional)

2. Crush the juniper berries, coriander seeds, and other optional spices using a mortar and pestle or a spice grinder. You want a coarse texture, not a powder.

3. Combine the neutral grain spirit and all the botanicals (including the crushed juniper berries, coriander seeds, and optional spices) in the large glass jar. Make sure the botanicals are fully submerged in the spirit.

4. Seal the jar and let it sit in a cool, dark place for 24 to 48 hours. This process is called steeping or maceration. Shake the jar gently every few hours to help the botanicals release their flavors.

5. After steeping, prepare your filter by placing the fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth over the funnel. Position the funnel over the clean 750 ml glass bottle.

6. Carefully pour the mixture from the jar through the strainer into the bottle. This process will filter out and separate the solid botanicals from the infused spirit.

7. Press down on the solids in the strainer or cheesecloth to extract any remaining liquid and flavors. Discard the solids.

8. Taste your homemade gin. If you're happy with the flavor, you can store the bottle in a cool, dark place. If you want a stronger flavor, you can add more botanicals to the jar, let it steep for another 24 to 48 hours, and then repeat the filtering process.

9. Dilute your gin with water to reach your desired alcohol by volume (ABV), usually around 40% to 45%. Keep in mind that the final ABV will depend on the strength of your neutral grain spirit and how much water you add.
