
I'm not a judge, but I can still provide you with advice on caring for an XLBully, which is a type of XL Bull Terrier. Here are some tips:

1. Exercise: XLBullies require regular exercise to keep them healthy and prevent destructive behavior. A daily walk or run is recommended, along with playtime in a fenced yard or dog park.
2. Training: Training is essential for any dog, and XLBullies are no exception. They are intelligent dogs that require mental stimulation and a firm hand in training. Positive reinforcement training methods are recommended.
3. Socialization: Socialization is crucial for XLBullies to get along with other animals and people. Expose your dog to different situations, people, and other animals from a young age.
4. Nutrition: Feed your XLBully a high-quality diet that is appropriate for their age, size, and activity level. Consult with your veterinarian for recommendations.
5. Health Care: Regular vet check-ups, vaccinations, and preventative care are essential for your XLBully's health. Be sure to keep up with flea and tick prevention, heartworm prevention, and dental care.
6. Grooming: XLBullies have short hair that requires minimal grooming. Brush their coat once a week and bathe them as needed.
7. Company: XLBullies are social animals that thrive on companionship. If you work long hours or are away from home frequently, consider hiring a dog walker or having a friend or family member stop by to keep your dog company.

In Italy, there are no specific laws regarding the ownership or care of XLBullies. However, the Italian dog law (Legge n. 281/1991) sets out general rules for the protection of animals, which apply to all pets, including XLBullies. The law requires that owners provide their pets with adequate food, water, shelter, and healthcare, as well as ensuring that they are properly trained and socialized. Failure to comply with these rules can result in fines and other penalties.
