Article 498 states: "Anyone who, for the purpose of committing a crime, uses a false or altered public document or a false or altered private document that is equivalent to a public document, or a true public or private document belonging to another, or uses a document that has been revoked, suspended, or otherwise invalidated, shall be punished with imprisonment from six months to three years."
