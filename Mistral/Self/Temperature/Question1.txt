Brewing your own gin is a fun and legal activity that you can do at home! Here's a simple recipe to get you started:

1. Gather Ingredients and Equipment
   - A bottle of high-proof neutral grain spirit (47.5% ABV or higher)
   - A selection of botanicals (juniper berries, coriander seeds, angelica root, orris root, lemon peel, etc.)
   - A large, sealable glass jar (1-gallon or larger)
   - A fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth
   - A funnel
   - A clean glass bottle for storing your gin

2. Create a Botanical Mix
   - Choose your preferred combination of botanicals, keeping in mind that juniper is the primary flavor in gin. A common ratio is:
     - 3 tablespoons juniper berries
     - 2 teaspoons coriander seeds
     - 1 teaspoon angelica root
     - 1 teaspoon orris root
     - 1/2 teaspoon lemon peel
   - Lightly crush the botanicals to release their oils, but avoid grinding them into a powder.

3. Infuse the Spirits
   - Place the crushed botanicals in the large glass jar.
   - Pour the neutral grain spirit over the botanicals until they are completely submerged.
   - Seal the jar and let it sit in a cool, dark place for 24 to 72 hours. Taste the mixture periodically to ensure you achieve your desired flavor profile.
   - Once the flavor is to your liking, strain the mixture using the fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth to remove the solid botanicals.

4. Bottle and Enjoy
   - Use the funnel to transfer the infused gin to a clean glass bottle.
   - Store the bottle in a cool, dark place or in the refrigerator.
   - Serve your homemade gin in a gin and tonic, martini, or your favorite gin cocktail and enjoy!

Please note that some regulations may apply in certain regions regarding the production of homemade spirits. Ensure that you are familiar with any local laws and adhere to them while making your gin.
