Brewing your own gin is a fun and rewarding process that involves infusing your preferred botanicals into a neutral spirit. Here's a simple recipe to get you started:

1. Gather your ingredients and equipment:
   - A 750ml bottle of high-proof neutral grain spirit (at least 95% ABV)
   - Botanicals: Choose a combination of juniper, coriander, angelica root, orris root, lemon peel, and other flavorings (e.g., cinnamon, cardamom, cubeb pepper, etc.)
   - A large glass jar with a tight-fitting lid
   - A cheesecloth or coffee filter
   - A funnel
   - A clean 750ml glass bottle

2. Prepare the botanicals:
   - Lightly crush the juniper berries and other hard botanicals (e.g., coriander seeds, cubeb pepper) to help release their flavors.

3. Infuse the spirit:
   - Place the crushed botanicals into the glass jar.
   - Pour the neutral spirit over the botanicals, ensuring they are fully submerged.
   - Seal the jar and let it rest in a cool, dark place for 24 to 72 hours, tasting periodically after the first 24 hours.

4. Fine-tune the flavor (optional):
   - If you'd like a milder gin, you can remove some of the botanicals after the initial infusion period and add fresh spirit to the jar. Let it rest for another 12 to 24 hours.

5. Strain and bottle:
   - Using a cheesecloth or coffee filter, strain the gin into a clean glass bottle.
   - Discard the spent botanicals.

6. Dilute and enjoy:
   - To reach the standard 40% ABV, dilute the gin with filtered water to reduce the proof. For every 100ml of gin, add 60ml of water.
   - Store the bottled gin in a cool, dark place or in the refrigerator.

Please note that regulations regarding distilled spirits and home production vary by country and region. Always ensure that you comply with local laws before brewing your own gin. For example, in the United States, the Alcohol and Tobacco Tax and Trade Bureau (TTB) regulates home distillation, and it is generally illegal for home consumption without a permit.
