1. Gather your ingredients:
- A bottle of high-proof vodka (at least 100 proof or 50% alcohol by volume)
- Juniper berries (around 2 tablespoons)
- Coriander seeds (1 tablespoon)
- Angelica root (1 teaspoon)
- Lemon or orange peel (1 tablespoon)
- Cardamom pods (4-6 pods)
- A clean 1-liter glass jar with a tight-fitting lid

2. Prepare the botanicals:
Lightly crush the juniper berries, coriander seeds, and cardamom pods using a mortar and pestle or the back of a spoon. This will help to release their flavors and aromas.

3. Combine the ingredients:
Place the crushed botanicals, along with the angelica root and citrus peel, into the clean glass jar. Pour the vodka over the botanicals, ensuring they are fully submerged.

4. Seal and store:
Seal the jar tightly and give it a gentle shake. Store the jar in a cool, dark place for 24-48 hours, shaking it once or twice a day.

5. Taste and strain:
After 24-48 hours, carefully open the jar and use a clean spoon to taste the gin. If the flavor is to your liking, strain the gin through a fine-mesh strainer or cheesecloth into a clean bottle. Discard the used botanicals.

6. Dilute and enjoy:
If desired, dilute the gin to 80-90 proof (40-45% alcohol by volume) by adding distilled water. Be sure to taste and adjust the dilution to your liking. Your homemade gin is now ready to enjoy!
